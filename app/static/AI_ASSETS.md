# ClimateLens generated media attribution

This file identifies generated media in the source repository. The website also displays an `AI-GENERATED` label over the concept art and beside the optional sound controls. These labels are part of the page UI; the original WebP pixel data remains unchanged.

## AI-generated illustrations

- `assets/singapore-island.webp` — concept art for the standard investigation page.
- `lens/img/sky.webp`, `lens/img/cloud.webp`, and `lens/img/{singapore,kuala-lumpur,bangkok,ho-chi-minh-city,jakarta}.webp` — sky and floating city islands.
- `lens/singapore/img/island.webp` and `lens/singapore/img/rock-{1,2}.webp` — Singapore Island Lab concept art.

These images are illustrative and are not NASA imagery, geographic maps, or measurements.

## AI-generated music

`lens/audio/home.mp3` and `lens/audio/{singapore,kuala-lumpur,bangkok,ho-chi-minh-city,jakarta}.mp3` are AI-generated with Google Flow Music (Lyria). The audio files retain their original untagged MP3 frames for seamless looping. The player is muted by default and visibly identifies the AI-generated music on both pages; this manifest records the origin of each track. The audio contains no climate data.

## Other assets

The 12 photos in `lens/singapore/img/landmarks/` are credited Wikimedia Commons photographs, not AI-generated. The `lens/singapore/data/modis/` images are NASA satellite imagery and must not carry an AI-generated label. The charts display NASA POWER data and must not carry an AI-generated label either. Retain their separate source and licence information.
